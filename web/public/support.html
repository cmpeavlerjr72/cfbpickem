CFB Pick'em — Support

CFB Pick'em is a free college football pick tracker for the 2026 FBS season.

Need help? Email cmpeavlerjr@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Including your device model and the app version helps.

How do I make picks?

Open My Picks and tap a team to pick them straight up. Tap again to unselect. Switch between weeks using the pills at the top. You can change a pick any time before that game kicks off.

When do picks lock?

Each pick locks automatically at that game's kickoff time. Games in the same week lock independently, so a Saturday-night game stays open after the noon games have started.

Why is my Results tab empty?

Results appear once games have been played and graded. The 2026 season kicks off August 29 — before then, every game shows as upcoming and there is nothing to grade yet. Once games go final, your picks grade automatically from live scores.

My scores aren't updating

Scores come from a public sports-data feed and need an internet connection. If scores look stale, check your connection and reopen the app. Already-graded results are cached on your device and keep working offline.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Your picks are stored locally on your device.

Will my picks transfer to a new phone?

Not currently — picks live in the app's local storage on a single device, so deleting the app or switching phones starts you fresh. Account-based sync is something we may add in a future version.

Is this gambling?

No. There is no real money, no entry fee, and no prizes anywhere in the app. It is a personal pick tracker.
