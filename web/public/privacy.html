CFB Pick'em — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

The short version

CFB Pick'em does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your picks and cached game results are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network requests

The app requests college football schedules and scores from publicly available sports-data endpoints. These requests do not include any personal information or identifiers beyond what any standard internet request contains (such as your IP address, which we never see or store — the request goes directly from your device to the data provider).

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app adds features that involve data collection (for example, online leagues), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described.
